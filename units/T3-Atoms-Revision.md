# T3 — Atoms revision

**Class:** `T3` Developing Science · two 50-minute singles.
**Today:** Week 2 Thursday P3. **Tomorrow:** Week 2 Friday P7 (14:10–15:00,
last lesson of the week). These are the final two slots of the fortnight.

| # | Lesson | Demand |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Atoms: the words and the picture | Recognise, match, label, write |
| 2 | Atoms: say it | Produce, speak, answer aloud |

Thursday is recognition and writing. Friday is speaking — which is both the
right way round for difficulty and the right thing for a Friday afternoon.

---

## Read this before building either one

**Open all three previous decks first.** Runtime, vocabulary, gestures and
sentence frames all come from the files, not from the template:

- `reference/Atoms L1.pptx` — atom, matter, tiny, part
- `reference/Drawing An Atom.pptx` — centre, outside
- `reference/The Nucleus.pptx` — nucleus, proton, neutron (electron re-drilled)

**No new words. None.** Revision for this group means retrieval, not addition.
If a word is not already in one of those three decks, it does not appear.

**Reuse everything.** Same pictures, same TPR gestures, same sentence frames,
same syllable splits. A familiar picture is what makes retrieval possible —
a new picture of the same thing makes it harder, not easier.

**Raise the demand, not the content.** The progression across the two lessons:
point → match → copy → complete a frame → say it unprompted. Same material at
each step, more required of them.

**British spelling: centre.** The unit plan writes "center". It is a planning
document and students never see it. Settled; do not raise it again.

**Friday P7 is the last lesson of the week.** Standing up, moving, speaking.
Do not put a long written task there.

## Lesson 1 — today

```
CLASS:      T3
SLOT:       single — Week 2 Thursday P3
TOPIC:      Atoms: the words and the picture

OBJECTIVES:
  1. Match all the unit words to their pictures.
  2. Draw an atom and label the centre and the outside.
  3. Write the sentence frames from the unit.

PREVIOUS:   reference/The Nucleus.pptx — and read Atoms L1 and Drawing An Atom
            as well. This revises all three.
THEY FOUND HARD: <fill in — pronunciation of electron and nucleus last time>

AVOID:      Any new vocabulary. Any picture they have not seen before.
```

Every word from the unit, in the order taught: **atom, matter, tiny, part,
centre, outside, nucleus, proton, neutron, electron.**

Run the drill routine on the three they find hardest rather than all ten — the
speaker notes from the previous lessons say which those are.

The You Do is a labelled diagram: draw the atom, label centre and outside, mark
where the proton, neutron and electron go. That is the assessment task from the
unit plan, rehearsed.

## Lesson 2 — tomorrow

```
CLASS:      T3
SLOT:       single — Week 2 Friday P7 (last lesson of the week)
TOPIC:      Atoms: say it

OBJECTIVES:
  1. Say where each part of an atom is, using a preposition of place.
  2. Complete the frame "An atom has a [part] in the [location]."
  3. Answer a spoken question about a diagram without a written prompt.

PREVIOUS:   reference/<Lesson 1 of this revision>.pptx
THEY FOUND HARD: <fill in>

AVOID:      Long written tasks. This is the last lesson of the week and the
            objectives are all spoken.
```

The two structures from the unit plan, drilled properly:

- **Prepositions of place** — "The centre is inside the atom." "The electron is
  outside."
- **The frame** — "An atom has a [part] in the [location]."

Most of the minutes go on **pairs**. Whole class, then half, then pairs, then
individuals. Pairs is where every student actually speaks; a whole-class round
gives each of them one turn.

Objective 3 is the oral assessment from the unit plan: point at a diagram, wait,
they answer. Rehearse it exactly as it will be done, so the assessment itself is
familiar rather than a new format.

End on a round where every answer is correct, so the week finishes with the
whole room succeeding out loud.
